# 06. Recruiter InMail & LinkedIn Outreach Synthesis

## 1. High-Conversion InMail Pitch Architecture

Recruiters and hiring managers spend an average of $6–10\text{ seconds}$ skimming cold outreach. The Pitch Studio produces a concise $90–120\text{ word}$ message structured for conversion:

```text
Hi [Recruiter Name] — I saw {Company} is expanding the team for the {Role} role and wanted to reach out directly.

I specialize in high-performance ML systems ({Top Skills}) with verifiable production metrics—including {Project 1 Impact}.

Given {Company}'s ambitious engineering goals, I would love to connect and discuss how my distributed systems and kernel optimization background can add immediate velocity to your team.

Best,
{Full Name}
```

---

## 2. InMail Synthesis Evaluation Criteria

* **Word Count**: Strictly kept between 80 and 130 words.
* **Metric Hook**: Emphasizes quantitative efficiency (e.g. *reducing P99 latency by 45%*).
* **Call to Action**: Low-friction closing invitation to connect.
