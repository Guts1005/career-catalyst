# 08. Counter-Offer Script Synthesis Specification

## 1. Negotiation Script Structure

The negotiation generator produces high-leverage responses tailored to the candidate's chosen leverage angle:

1. **Competing Offers from Frontier Labs**: Cites parallel interview momentum and competing total compensation packages.
2. **Market Benchmark Percentiles**: References 90th percentile Level 6/Principal compensation surveys for San Francisco AI labs.
3. **Specialized GPU/Triton Skillset Scarcity**: Emphasizes proven capacity to optimize low-level CUDA/Triton kernels and save multi-million dollar GPU training compute.

---

## 2. Quantitative Script Delta Formula

$$\Delta_{\text{negotiation}} = \text{Target Total Comp} - (\text{Base} + \text{Equity} + \text{Bonus})$$

Example:
* Initial Offer: $\$235\text{k} + \$180\text{k} + \$45\text{k} = \$460\text{k}$
* Target Comp: $\$505\text{k}$
* Generated Delta: $+\$45,000\text{/year}$
